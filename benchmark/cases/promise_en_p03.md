# PROMISE NFR (English) — Project 3

## Functional Requirements

### F001: A cohort shall be associated to one Program of Study offered by the department.

### F002: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a listing of students to a cohort.

### F003: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a single student to a cohort

### F004: Students added to a cohort shall be eligible for the cohorts Program of Study.

### F005: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to specify the preferred sequence of classes for a given cohort.

### F006: For each class within a sequence for a cohort  Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to specify which quarter that class will be offered.

### F007: For each class within a sequence for a cohort  Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to specify which quarter that class will be offered.

### F008: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add new classes to a sequence of classes

### F009: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add clinical classes or sections to a sequence of classes

### F010: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a new cohort to the system  identified by start month and year.

### F011: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add new classes for the nursing department into the system.

### F012: A class shall be either a non-clinical class or a clinical class.

### F013: A non-clinical class shall specify the course name  lecture room requirements  and instructor needs.

### F014: A clinical class shall specify the course name  lecture room requirements  clinical site needs  lecture instructor needs  and clinical lab instructor needs.

### F015: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall be able to create a new Program of Study.

### F016: A Program of Study shall consist of a program name and listing of required classes (both clinical and non-clinical) that must be completed.

### F017: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to specify which classes are required for a Program of Study

### F018: The system shall be able to display a report of needed classes for a given quarter for all cohorts of all programs for Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members planning purposes.

### F019: The report of needed classes shall include (but not be limited to) classes to be offered  number of sections needed  number of labs needed  and room types needed.

### F020: Classes for a given cohort shall not conflict with regards to the time and day that they are offered.

### F021: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a new clinical site into the system.

### F022: Clinical site information shall include (but not be limited to) the name of the site  contact person  and contact information.

### F023: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a new clinical lab section for an existing clinical class into the System.

### F024: A clinical lab section shall include the clinical site name  the class  instructor  day and time of the lab.

### F025: Program Administrators and Nursing Staff Members shall be able to add a student who has registered for a clinical class to a clinical lab section for that class.

### F026: The system shall allow a Program Administrator or Nursing staff member to remove a student from a clinical lab section.

### F027: The system shall allow a Program Administrator/Nursing Staff Member to move a student from one clinical lab section to another clinical lab section corresponding to the same clinical class.

### F028: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall be able to cancel a clinical lab section only if there are no students registered for that clinical lab section.

### F029: A Program Administrator/Nursing Staff Member shall be able to add a new nursing student to the system.

### F030: A staff member shall have the ability to manage the progress of a part time student  which includes modifying their sequence of classes and timeline of classes.

### F031: The system shall be able to display a printable summary for individual cohorts  which will include the students enlisted  the Program of study  sequence of classes  cohort progress through the program  and timeline of completion.

### F032: The system shall be able to display a printable summary for individual clinical sites  which will include information on labs being held at that site for the given quarter (Clinical site  general information on labs held at that location  dates and times of all labs held there).

### F033: The system shall be able to display a printable summary for individual clinical labs  which will include (but not be limited to) class name  class number  lab section  listing of students enrolled  instructor  day(s)  time  and location.

### F034: The system shall be able to display a printable summary for individual nursing students  which will include (but not be limited to) student name  student ID  admission date  classes  credits  GPA  and the cohort that the student is enrolled in.

### F035: The system shall be able to display a printable summary of the system log.

### F036: The system shall contain contact information (e-mail and phone number) for all people relevant to the system  including (but not limited to) staff members  students  lecture instructors  clinical lab instructors  and clinical site administrators.

### F037: The system will use the stored e-mail addresses as a primary means of communicating information to affected parties.

### F038: The system will notify affected parties for routine communication purposes  including but not limited to informing students of upcoming offered classes (timeline) and what their schedule will be/shall be.

### F039: The system will notify affected parties when changes occur affecting clinicals  including but not limited to clinical section capacity changes  and clinical section cancellations.

### F040: The system will notify affected parties when changes occur affecting classes  including but not limited to class cancellations  class section detail changes  and changes to class offerings for a given quarter.

### F041: The system will notify affected parties when changes occur affecting cohorts  including but not limited to changes to the sequence for a cohorts program of study and changes to a given weeks schedule (lab cancelled this week due to instructor illness).

### F042: The system will notify affected parties when changes occur affecting specific students  including but not limited to changing a students status from full time to part time and moving a student to a different clinical section.

### F043: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to modify information relating to cohorts  including cohort identifier  program of study  preferred sequence of classes and quarters that a cohort will be taking specific classes.

### F044: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to modify information relating to a Program of Study within the Nursing Department  including the Program of study name  and required classes for that Program of Study.

### F045: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to modify information relating to a Clinical Site  including the clinical site name  site contact person  contact information  and address.

### F046: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to modify information relating to a Clinical Lab Section  including the clinical site name  the department/section for the lab  the class  instructor  day and time of lab  and contact information.

### F047: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall have the ability to modify information relating to a Nursing Student  including student ID  student name  phone number  e-mail  status (full time or part time)  and program of study.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### N001 [Usability]: The system shall be easy to use by the Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members.

### N002 [Usability]: All of the users shall be able to use the system after one day of training.

### N003 [Usability]: The system shall help the user avoid making mistakes while scheduling classes and clinicals for the nursing students.

### N004 [Usability]: The number of mistakes noted by the students shall be decreased by 50% in the first year.

### N005 [Usability]: The system shall be intuitive to the Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members.

### N006 [Usability]: 2 out of 3 Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members shall successfully be able to use the system to manage the scheduling of classes and clinicals.

### N007 [Usability]: The system shall use symbols and words that are naturally understandable by the user community.

### N008 [Usability]: The verbiage used within the system shall be consistent with Standard English and the terminology of the national Nursing Community.

### N009 [Performance]: Staff members shall be able to complete a set of tasks in a timely manner.

### N010 [Performance]: The response time of general student management tasks shall take no longer than 5 seconds and the response time of schedule generation shall take no longer than 30 seconds.

### N011 [Availability]: The system shall be available for use between the hours of 8am and 6pm.

### N012 [Availability]: The system shall achieve 95% up time.

### N013 [Scalability]: system shall be able to handle all of the user requests/usage during business hours.

### N014 [Scalability]: The system shall cater to 10 simultaneous users from 9am to 5pm.

### N015 [Scalability]: The system shall be capable of processing 100% of nursing students and their classes for the next 10 years.

### N016 [Scalability]: The system shall be expected to manage the nursing program curriculum and class/clinical scheduling for a minimum of 5 years.

### N017 [Operational]: The system shall able to operate within a business office environment typical of the Nursing Department at DePaul University.

### N018 [Operational]: The system shall be used within the specifications defined by the computers used by the Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members.

### N019 [Operational]: The system shall operate within the Windows XP Professional operating system.

### N020 [Operational]: The system shall interface with CampusConnects central server

### N021 [Operational]: The system shall interface with the faculty central server.

### N022 [Operational]: The system shall interface with the student master server.

### N023 [Operational]: We must be able to interface with any HTML browser.

### N024 [Security]: Only authorized users shall have access to clinical site information.

### N025 [Security]: Program Administrators/Nursing Staff Members are the only people who shall have access to clinical site details.

### N026 [Security]: Only authorized users shall have access to students personal information.

### N027 [Security]: Dr Susan Poslusny and Dr Julie Donalek are the only people who shall have access to students personal information.

### N028 [Security]: Only authorized users shall have access to the portion of the system that interfaces with CampusConnect.

### N029 [Security]: Fit Criterion: Dr Susan Poslusny and Karen Sysol are the only people who shall have access to the final class section scheduling for the system that interfaces with CampusConnect.

### N030 [Security]: The system shall have basic data integrity checking to reduce the possibility of incorrect or invalid data being introduced.

### N031 [Security]: The system shall protect private information in accordance with the organizations information policy.

### N032 [Security]: The system shall be built such that it is as secure as possible from malicious interference.

### N033 [Security]: The system shall use the organizations standard virus protection software to help monitor threats to itself.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### N001 [Usability]: The system shall be easy to use by callers and supervisors. Callers and supervisors must be able to accomplish any system task within 2 minutes.

### N002 [Usability]: The system shall be easy to learn by callers and supervisors. Callers and supervisors shall learn how to use the system within 10 minutes.

### N003 [Performance]: The system shall be responsive. At the DePaul TeleServices Center  when a supervisor uses the system to view a callers schedule  the system response time must be no more than 2 seconds 90% of the time and no more than 5 seconds the rest of the time.

### N004 [Performance]: On a 56k connection  the system response time for all operations must be no more than 6 seconds 90% of the time.

### N005 [Performance]: On a 112k connection or faster  the system response time for all operations must be no more than 3 seconds 90% of the time.

### N006 [Availability]: The system shall have high availability every day of the year.The system must be available for use between 12:00AM and 6:00PM all days of the year.

### N007 [Availability]: The system must be available for use between 7:00AM and 11:59PM all days of the year.

### N008 [Maintainability]: The product shall be internet browser independent.The product must run using Internet Explorer and Netscape Navigator.

### N009 [Security]: The product shall ensure that only supervisors can advertise empty time slots.The product must ensure that supervisors are allowed to access advertise empty time slots.

### N010 [Security]: The product shall ensure that only supervisors can enter a callers schedule.The product must ensure that supervisors are allowed to access advertise empty time slots.

### N011 [Security]: The product shall ensure that only supervisors can view schedule of all callers.The product must ensure that supervisors are allowed to access advertise empty time slots.

### N012 [Look & Feel]: The interface shall have a simple look. In the interface and usability survey  90% of beta testers must report that the interface is adequately simple.

### N013 [Look & Feel]: The interface shall be appealing to callers and supervisors. In the interface and usability survey  80% of beta testers must report that the interface is appealing.

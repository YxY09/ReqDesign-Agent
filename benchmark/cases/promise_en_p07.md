# PROMISE NFR (English) — Project 7

## Functional Requirements

### F001: The System shall allow generation of Inventory Quantity Adjustment documents on demand.

### F002: The System shall allow on demand generation of all Inventory Quantity Adjustment documents since certain point of time.

### F003: The IQA subsystem shall take into account current inventory status and adjust inventory of substitutionary ingredients accordingly.

### F004: The System shall allow entering  storing and modifying product formulas.

### F005: Product formulas shall allow use of semi-products  which have own formulas.

### F006: Product formula ingredients shall allow defining substitutionary ingredients.

### F007: Product formula substitutionary ingredients shall be entered automatically by the PFE subsystem.

### F008: Formula substitutionary ingredients shall be kept in persisted order

### F009: Product shall allow entering  storing and modifying product menus used by POS terminals.

### F010: PME Subsystem shall allow building product menus based on the list of products kept in the accounting system.

### F011: PME Subsystem shall allow keeping submenus within menus and products within submenus in a managed and persisted order.

### F012: PME Subsystem shall allow for a product to exist in more than one submenu

### F013: The RMS System shall have Windows XP look and feel and use GUI futures making its use intuitive.

### F014: The System shall generate Inventory Quantity Adjustment document based on product daily sales data and product formulas.

### F015: The System shall generate Inventory Quantity Adjustment document automatically when daily Product Sales data is available.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### N001 [Performance]: Recreation of Inventory Quantity Adjustment for the period of previous 30 days shall take no more than 30min.

### N002 [Performance]: The System shall allow for a minimum of  6 users to work at the same time.

### N003 [Operational]: The System shall utilize currently owned computer equipment.

### N004 [Operational]: The System shall be able to automatically backup business data and restore them on demand.

### N005 [Security]: The System shall maintain consistent security.  Any changes in user passwords and/or permissions should take affect in all parts of the System.

### N006 [Security]: The System shall granularly control access rights to all major subsystems.  It should be possible to assign access rights to all subsystems independently.

### N007 [Legal]: The System shall meet all applicable accounting standards.  The final version of the System must successfully pass independent audit performed by a certified auditor.

### N008 [Operational]: The System shall not require additional third party licenses resulting in royalty fees.

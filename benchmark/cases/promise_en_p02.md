# PROMISE NFR (English) — Project 2

## Functional Requirements

### F001: The system shall allow a real estate agent to query MLS information

### F002: The user shall be able to get driving directions to a selected property

### F003: The user shall be able to download appointments and contact information for clients

### F004: The system shall display clear property images in the search results

### F005: The system shall update or create new property listings in the MLS

### F006: Realtor shall be able to generate a CMA report based on property criteria

### F007: The system shall be able to call the seller or buyer to schedule an appointment

### F008: The realtor shall be notified of new client appointments after automatic synchronization with office system

### F009: The realtor shall be able to retrieve a map showing property locations for a specified area

### F010: The system shall notify the realtor when a seller or buyer responds to an appointment request

### F011: The product shall allow the user to save the property search results.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### N001 [Look & Feel]: The look and feel of the system shall conform to the user interface standards of the smart device.

### N002 [Look & Feel]: The user interface shall have standard menus  buttons for navigation

### N003 [Look & Feel]: The system shall have a professional appearance

### N004 [Look & Feel]: The product shall have a consistent color scheme and fonts.

### N005 [Usability]: The system shall be used by realtors with no training.

### N006 [Usability]: The system shall be intuitive and self explanatory.

### N007 [Usability]: The product shall be easy for a realtor to learn.

### N008 [Usability]: The product shall use symbols and words that are naturally understandable by the realtor community.

### N009 [Performance]: The product shall produce search results in an acceptable time

### N010 [Performance]: The search results shall be returned no later 30      seconds  after the user has entered the search criteria

### N011 [Performance]: The product shall generate a CMA report in an acceptable time.

### N012 [Performance]: The CMA report shall be returned no later 60 seconds after the user has entered the CMA report criteria.

### N013 [Performance]: The product shall synchronize contacts and appointments in an acceptable time.

### N014 [Performance]: The product shall synchronize with the office system every hour.

### N015 [Availability]: The product shall be available for use 24 hours per day  365 days  per year

### N016 [Availability]: Out of 1000 accesses to the system  the system is available 999 times.

### N017 [Fault Tolerance]: The product shall operate in offline mode whenever internet connection is unavailable.

### N018 [Fault Tolerance]: The product shall allow the user to view previously downloaded search results  CMA reports  and appointments.

### N019 [Fault Tolerance]: The product shall retain user preferences in the event of a failure.

### N020 [Fault Tolerance]: 100% of saved user preferences shall be restored when system comes back online.

### N021 [Scalability]: The product shall be able to support multiple remote users

### N022 [Scalability]: The product shall be able support 1000 simultaneous users.

### N023 [Scalability]: The product shall be capable of handling the existing 1000 users. This number is expected to grow 5 times within the next year.

### N024 [Usability]: The product shall be installed by an untrained realtor   without recourse to separately-printed instructions.

### N025 [Usability]: 90% of untrained realtors shall be able to install the product on their device without printed instructions

### N026 [Portability]: The product is expected to run on Windows CE and Palm operating systems.

### N027 [Security]: Only registered realtors shall be able to access the system.

### N028 [Security]: Every user of the system shall be authenticated and authorized.

### N029 [Security]: The product shall prevent its data from incorrect data being introduced.
